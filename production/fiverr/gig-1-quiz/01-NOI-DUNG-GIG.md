## Ô "Gig title" (tối đa 80 ký tự, Fiverr tự thêm "I will")
```
create engaging quiz and trivia videos for your YouTube channel
```
*Từ khóa chính "quiz video", "trivia video" nằm trong tiêu đề. Sau khi anh đếm "services available", nếu "trivia video" ít cạnh tranh hơn thì đảo lên trước.*

## Ô "FAQ"
1. **Can I provide my own questions?** — Yes. Send a sheet or a list; I'll format and fact-check them. Otherwise I write them.
2. **Which languages?** — English by default. Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Vietnamese available at the same price.
3. **Do you use copyrighted images?** — No. Visuals are AI-generated or licensed, so your channel stays safe.
4. **Can you match my existing videos?** — Yes. Send one of your videos and I'll copy the layout, timer style and pacing.
5. **What's the turnaround for 10 videos?** — 8 days standard, faster with the extra.

## Ô "Requirements" (câu hỏi khách phải trả lời khi đặt)
1. Your channel link (or a video you like the style of).
2. Quiz topic(s) and number of questions.
3. Logo + brand colors (optional).
4. Preferred voice: male / female, accent.
5. Horizontal (16:9), vertical (9:16) or both?
